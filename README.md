# Project 1: Voltage Division & KVL Verification (Series Resistors)

1. Overview & Objective
the objectice of this experiment project is to verify kvl  .


2. Circuit Setup & Components
    2 1k ohm resistors
    1 330 ohm resistor

### 3. Measurements & Results

| Parameter | Theory | Measured | Error |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source | 9 V | 9.29 V | +3.22% |
| R1 | 1000 ohm | 991 ohm | -0.9% |
| R2 | 330 ohm | 324 ohm | -1.82% |
| R3 | 1000 ohm | 987 ohm | -1.3% |
| R4 | 100 ohm | 98 ohm | -2% |
| V across R1 | 3.70 V | 3.83 V | +3.51% |
| V across R2 | 1.22 V | 1.24 V | +1.64% |
| V across R3 | 3.70 V | 3.80 V | +2.70% |
| V across R4 | 0.37 V | 0.37 V | 0% |
| Current | 3.7 mA | 3.83 mA | +3.51% |
